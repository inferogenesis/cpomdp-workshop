## Slide before Task 2 · What changes when observations depend on location?

**Task 1: Fixed sensor noise**
Sensor reliability is the same wherever the rocket travels.

**Task 2: Location-dependent noise**
Observations are more reliable near the beacon and noisier further away.

**An important change in the objective**
The rocket now has a preference over observations: it wants a reading that indicates it
is on the landing pad.

**Question for participants**
*Will the rocket change its route when some locations provide more reliable
observations?*

## Slide before Task 3 · What happens when observations become unreliable?

**The beacon**
Observations become more reliable as the rocket approaches the beacon.

**The blackout**
Observations become increasingly noisy towards the centre of the blackout.

**An additional challenge**
There is a rock inside the blackout, but none of the rockets has any representation of
it in its model.

**Question for participants**
*How might the rockets respond, and what can their trajectories tell us about their
behaviour?*

## Closing slide · A working model is the beginning, not the end

**What have we demonstrated?**
How changing the observation model and preferences can influence an agent's behaviour.

**What remains to be established?**
Whether those behaviours generalise, how robust they are, and which aspects of the
computational model have been independently validated.
